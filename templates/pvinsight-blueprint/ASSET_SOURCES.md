# Example asset sources

PV tracker, BESS, substation, transmission, data-center and site PNGs were rendered from the illustrative Blender geometry developed for this example. They are cropped transparent cutouts and are not project-specific engineering drawings. The optional generator is in extensions/blender.

The original PVInsight logos are from:

- https://pvinsightinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/PVinsight-Logo-white.png
- https://pvinsightinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/PVinsight-Logo_v3-01.png

They are included solely as branding for the PVInsight example. The generic engine and basic-motion template contain no company-specific branding or logic.
